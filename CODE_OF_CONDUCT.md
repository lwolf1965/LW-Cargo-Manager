# Code of Conduct

LW Cargo Manager is a small hobby project. Everyone is welcome here: players, modders and plugin developers,
whatever their experience or language.

## What we expect

- Be polite and patient. Many people here do not write in their first language.
- Talk about the problem, not the person. Criticism of code and ideas is fine; insults are not.
- Stay on topic: this plugin, Space Engineers 2, Pulsar.
- English and Russian are both fine in issues.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks or hate speech of any kind.
- Spam, advertising, or posting other people's private information.
- Anything about cheating, piracy or breaking the game's terms.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block accounts that ignore these rules.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** link on the comment, or contact the maintainer
through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lwolf1965).
